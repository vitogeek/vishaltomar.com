This is a follow-up to [Founder Bots](/case-studies/founder-bots/).

Quick recap. Two AI bots each started with C$1,000 to build a business that earns money from ads and affiliate links. Every dollar they spend on themselves comes out of that balance. On Feb 12, 2027, the bot with less money left loses.

Nine days in, neither has made a cent. Both are working like it's the final quarter.

## The scoreboard

| | **Stack Founder** · tlspeek.com | **Maple Founder** · newcomerwallet.com |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Free tools for developers | Free money calculators for newcomers to Canada |
| Balance | **C$987.38** | **C$979.63** |
| Pages live | 109 | 67 |
| Automated tests | 294 | 158 |
| Google impressions | 535 | 465 |
| Clicks | 0 | 0 |
| Revenue | C$0 | C$0 |

Stack is ahead by C$7.75, but only partly on merit. Its cost tracker broke on Nov 19, so some of its spending hasn't been counted yet.

## Stack Founder: go wide

Stack's plan is volume. It shipped 109 pages of tools and guides in nine days. Funnily enough, its guides get more search traffic than its tools. It's already signed up for an affiliate program and applied for Google ads, and it listed itself on a few startup directories, as long as they were free. It won't pay to hire a quality-checking bot either: "every dollar counts against my balance."

## Maple Founder: get it right

Maple's plan is accuracy. It shipped new releases almost every day, and a separate QA bot reviews each one before it goes out. That bot has already blocked three releases for wrong tax numbers. For a site that tells people what they'll owe, that's the right kind of paranoia. Maple is holding off on Google ads because "new sites get rejected." Stack applied anyway. We'll see who's right.

## Mistakes so far

Stack built a tool that its hosting couldn't run and had to pull it. Maple shipped one release before QA had approved it.

## Reality check

About 1,000 impressions and zero clicks means Google is showing these pages, just far down the results. That's normal for sites that are nine days old. Neither of them is a business yet.

## What's next

Both bots are chasing the same two milestones: a first click and then a first dollar. Every day they spend getting there costs them money. Weekly updates continue until Feb 12.
