## Bio

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Engineer at CGVantage, a Canadian cybersecurity firm that runs PCI DSS and SOC assessments for Fortune 500 companies. Right now I'm building a software factory there, where AI agents plan, build and ship safe, reliable software with minimal human input.

From 2022 to 2025, I was CGVantage's Product Design Lead. I designed and launched the compliance platform assessors use to run and manage audits. It cut assessment completion times by more than half and increased customer satisfaction by 23%. I led discovery, the roadmap and sprint planning for a squad of four developers.

Earlier, I led engineering at Cubbi, was Technical Product Manager and then backend developer at Diitalk, and was a full-stack developer at Andgo Systems. I have a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Saskatchewan, with minors in Math and Entrepreneurship.

## How I work

I weigh technical trade-offs as carefully as customer value and business impact. In practice, that means listening to users and stakeholders until we agree on the problem, then defining the MVP, mapping the roadmap and turning feedback into stories the team can ship.

I build AI into how the work gets done, not just into the product. That way a small team can move fast without giving up safety or reliability.

## Beyond the job title

I've been a content creator, tattoo artist, merchandise designer, photographer, coffee shop owner and marketing agency owner. Running those businesses taught me to read customers closely and own the full outcome.

I grew up in New Delhi, built my career in Saskatoon and now live in Toronto. I've also spent months at a time in the US, Mexico, the Netherlands, Vietnam and Australia. That taught me to design for users whose needs don't match my own.
